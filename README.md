## Overview
PMI is a research and development project exploring how Artificial Intelligence can support construction project management by identifying, interpreting and tracing the potential consequences of architectural design changes. 
Construction projects involve interconnected information across 3D models, material specifications, quantities, budgets and construction schedules. When a design changes, understanding it's downstream effects often requires manually reviewing multiple datasets and project documents. 
PMI investigates how AI can improve this process by connecting architectural model information with project management data to support more informed decision-making.

## Research Question
How can Artificial Intelligence support the identification and interpretation of downstream impacts caused by architectural design changes within BIM-based-construction workflows?

## The Problem
Architectural design modifications can create a chain reaction across construction processes. 
For example, changing a wall's material specification may affect :
- Material quantities and procurement requirements 
- Construction costs
- Installation activities and dependencies
- Project Scheduling
- Coordination between project stakeholders.

**PMI focuses on bridging the gap between design modifications and project management decisions.**

## Proposed Solution 
PMI proposes an AI-assisted workflow that begins with a 3D architectural model and investigates the impact of modifications on connected information.

The system is intended to :
1. **Read BIM information:** Extract building elements, properties and relationships from IFC models.
2. **Identify changes:** Compare model versions of specified design modifications.
3. **Interpret changes:** Use AI to explain what has changed and identify relevant construction implications.
4. **Trace dependencies:** Connect modified elements with available material, quantity, cost and schedule information.
5. **Generate insights:** Produce structured, evidence-based change impact reports for project managers.

The initial prototype will focus on a limited set of architectural elements and changes scenarios.

## Why Artificial Intelligence?

BIM provides structured building information, but understanding the wider implications of design changes often requires interpreting information across multiple project sources.

PMI explores how AI can bridge this gap by connecting detected changes to relevant construction information and explaining their potential impacts on materials, costs, and schedules.

While Python and rule-based methods handle precise data extraction and change detection, AI can support contextual interpretation and generate understandable, evidence-based insights for project managers.

**Our goal is to transform BIM data into actionable project intelligence.**
