# Construction knowledge

Classify cast-in-place foundations, columns, beams and slabs using IFC class, material, location and an approved method. Join to approved work templates keyed by operation, family, zone, lift and pour. Templates specify measurable scope, quantity basis, sequencing constraints, allowable modes, approval, version and catalogue candidates. Record ambiguity or absent classification in a review queue. One slab may drive reinforcement, formwork, place, cure and strike. Several elements can contribute to a single pour, and one element may span pours via quantity allocations. Require conservation within each measurement and operation scope.

| Family | Formwork | Reinforcement | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 03ERM00001 | 03ACC00011 | 03HAZ80020 or 03HAA00010 |
| Column | 05HEM00001 | 05HAC00015 | 05HHP00003 |
| Beam | 05HEM00051 | 05HAC00015 | 05HHJ00003 |
| Slab | 05HEM00101 | 05HAC00015 or 05HAM00010 | 05HHL00003 |

Use the Stage 4.0B productivity coverage table as the candidate index. Contact area in m², reinforcement mass in kg and compatible net placed concrete volume in m³ have distinct takeoff bases. Require reviewed densities, bar schedules and geometric assumptions where needed. For each measured scope, select a bundled item or a mutually exclusive set of constituent resources and reconcile cost and hours separately. Inspect whether formwork includes striking before applying 05HED00001. Preserve physical striking activity and release constraints even when its demand or cost is allocated from a bundle. Curing lacks a universal catalogue duration and requires an approved specification and monitoring or release condition.

Engineers approve sequencing per method and zone. Templates can express pre-pour inspections and reinforcement/formwork completion, support and access constraints, curing from placement completion and strength-confirmed striking or reshoring. Branch by construction method where installation order varies. Unapproved sequencing, missing load-path information or unresolved strength criteria block publication of the affected schedule.
