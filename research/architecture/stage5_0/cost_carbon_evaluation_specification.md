# Cost and environmental evaluation

Compute a direct ledger by materials, worker-hours by trade and equipment-hour by class. Choose either a validated bundled direct item or its reconciled constituents for a scope. Keep site overhead schedules (per working shift or elapsed day as contracted) and one-off scenario increments such as mobilization or rental periods in distinct ledger lines. Specify currency, price date, region, escalation and tax/overheads policy. BCCA basis is Andalucía, Dec-2023 with mixed concept dates. Retain source price and document any approved localization. Incomplete cost coverage makes a hard budget verdict unavailable unless bounds prove compliance under a documented policy.

Use ÖKOBAUDAT 2024-III entries identified by UUID, version, stock, geographic applicability, declared unit and amount, lifecycle modules, indicator, EN 15804 amendment and characterization basis. Multiply normalized activity/quantity by rate per declared amount. Keep A1–A3 aggregates disjoint from constituent modules, A4/A5 distinct and module D separate. Keep A1 GWP and A2 GWP-total and components on distinct compatible bases. Disclose geographic mismatch and conversion assumptions. Prevent duplicate accounting between included EPD processes and separately modeled transport, installation energy or waste.

Design fixes installed quantities and specified materials for a given scenario family. Approved substitutions and revisions can change manufacturing impacts. Route/distance/load, equipment energy, site electricity, waste and formwork reuse can be planning-sensitive when supported. A shorter project alone provides no carbon credit. Reusable formwork systems and diesel fuel lack acquired coefficients and remain explicit gaps. Report covered subtotal, coverage by line and exclusions. Incomplete or asymmetric support blocks a full-impact comparison. Prefer common supported scope for comparisons and disclose sensitivity to unresolved items.
