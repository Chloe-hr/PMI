# Revision propagation

Compare immutable same-project snapshots. First join namespace-qualified IFC GlobalIds when unique and consistent with class and identity evidence. Duplicate IDs, incompatible classes and suspected reuse demand review. Compare normalized placement/geometry, properties, spatial container, material composition and quantity basis with versioned absolute and relative tolerances. For unmatched records, rank candidates by source crosswalk, family/type, location, dimensions and alignment-aware geometry. Keep candidate scores and disagreements. Approve ambiguous mappings and split/merge lineage manually. Until approval distinguish unresolved matches from confirmed added and removed elements.

Emit old/new references, changed fields, signed deltas for compatible units and measurement bases, and unresolved differences. A missing quantity is unknown. Traverse allocation links to affected operations and packages, then dependency/resource networks. Recompute affected demands, waits, estimates, schedules, cost and compatible carbon totals from the same reference and scenario assumptions for a design-only comparison. Report separate deltas for changes in assumptions. Compare CPM paths, float and resource-driven finish effects. Show review dependencies and invalidated approvals.

At an explicit status date keep completed actuals, sunk cost and work history fixed. Preserve ongoing actual starts, consumed work and resources, and schedule independently confirmed remaining scope subject to physical commitments. Route changed or deleted installed work to engineering disposition and create approved rework or demolition scope if required. Future activities can be replanned. Baselines and solver results are immutable, with new run IDs and parent links.
