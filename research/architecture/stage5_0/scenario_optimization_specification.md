# Scenario optimization

Decision variables include feasible activity starts, approved crew/equipment/method modes, eligible approved zone order and permitted procurement options. Engineering safety and strength holds, structural and sequencing rules, time calendars, shared capacity, commitments, material availability and confirmed bounds apply identically to each objective.

Economic objective minimizes supported direct costs + elapsed/working site overhead according to its basis + scenario additions. Environmental objective minimizes supported decision-sensitive impacts for a selected compatible indicator and module set, with fixed design impacts displayed separately. Applicability requires comparable coverage across modes. Quantize coefficients and publish scale and maximum error. Feasibility objective can lexicographically minimize preferred-date tardiness, minimize worst tardiness under a planner-approved set of duration or capacity stress cases, then minimize peak utilization or maximize approved buffers. Stress case replay distinguishes nominal feasibility from measured robustness. If robust feasibility is declared hard, enforce all required stress cases.

Use explicit bounds or lexicographic tie-breakers to expose trade-offs, for example least supported cost within a carbon cap and preferred deadline. Report metrics, coverage, solver status and schedule differences. Deduplicate decisions and report ties or a single meaningful option when appropriate. Show baseline alongside candidates. User selection persists scenario, model/input digests, objective scope, coverage and approvals. Only a verified feasible selected schedule can be exported.
