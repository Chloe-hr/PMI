# Validation strategy

Use a three-level evidence ladder. Synthetic fixtures verify deterministic behavior and IFC4D read-back. Independent IFCs verify quantity completeness, extraction accuracy, coordinate alignment and real exporter diversity after Section A. Engineer-reviewed construction cases substantiate work mappings, safe sequencing, holds and feasible crews. Report fixtures, revision IDs, checksums, reference versions, thresholds, excluded scope and reviewer decisions per run.

Validate structural JSON and separately resolve IDs, dimensions, local calendars, unit conversion, allocation conservation and provenance graph. Test BCCA decomposition against published direct prices, trade-hours and duplicate-bundle cases. Test carbon inventory on incompatible unit, indicator and lifecycle cases and incomplete diesel/formwork scope. Hand-calculate simple work and CPM cases, enumerate small resource schedules and verify all solver outputs independently. Include resource overload, holiday/DST, curing across a weekend, strength hold, impossible deadline, timeout and horizon diagnostics. For revision, exercise regenerated and duplicated IDs, split/merge, material swaps, partial actuals and removed installed work. Validate IFC4D round trip for task times, dependencies, product assignments and original model fidelity. Run repeated comparisons for reproducibility.

Release thresholds for real takeoff are set with the engineering owner after Section A establishes measurement bases and tolerances. Block release on any known violation of hard constraints, broken provenance, unresolved critical quantity used in a schedule, or altered source bytes. Preserve Stage 3/4 entry points and evidence. No locked Cambridge parsing or existing-model inference is needed for these validations.
