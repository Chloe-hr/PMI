Condition: these values entered and approved by a PMI user per project; outputs labelled as catalogue-based estimates.

Supervised-learning eligibility

No dataset provides, for the same activity, a verified actual duration and the quantity, productivity and crew inputs needed for an engineering duration. The target y = log(actual/engineering) cannot be computed without merging unrelated sources, which this stage forbids.

Leakage: Thiele Final Qty, Final Cost, Final Rate, monthly AQ/AC/EV and %C claims are post-execution and must never be features.
