## Schedule consistency

All 47 planned and 47 synthetic actual durations, and all 141 version durations, are positive and match **inclusive calendar days**: finish - start + 1. Preserve that convention explicitly when converting to IFC datetimes/durations; do not silently apply working-day calendars. Progress percentages lie in [0,100], are monotonic per task, and reach 100 at synthetic actual finish.

However, dates and declared dependencies are not jointly consistent. All links are FS; for inclusive day dates with lag L, successor start must be at least predecessor finish + 1 + L days. Conflicts below include baseline and revision cases. Actual overlaps may represent out-of-sequence work, but the workbook provides no exception policy; do not silently repair or treat them as valid precedence compliance. Baseline V0 reproduces baseline conflicts.

## Suitability and Stage 3.8B decision

**NO-GO for unrestricted Stage 3.8B IFC4D conversion and schedule-validity acceptance.** GlobalId joins and complete mappings support conversion, and Gantt bars can be rendered, but rendering alone would conceal inconsistent precedence. The inputs are usable now as explicitly synthetic ingestion/geometry/linking and negative schedule-validation fixtures. They do not validate real construction execution or predictive accuracy.

Before accepting a valid-schedule IFC4D fixture: define inclusive-day and lag semantics; document the meaning of delay_days_synthetic; resolve or explicitly classify every baseline/revision dependency conflict in a derived, versioned copy; preserve originals and their hashes. Define how actual out-of-sequence work is represented. Then require round-trip checks of task IDs, schedule/version identity, dates, durations, IfcRelSequence relations and lags, and IfcRelAssignsToProcess element assignments. Proxy geometry is sufficient for association/animation smoke checks, not realistic construction-method validation. Do not infer planned tasks from synthetic actual/progress values or train on these records.

No originals were changed; no conversion, training, commit or push was performed.
